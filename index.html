VUSIC — MUSIC VISUALIZER GUIDE
GETTING STARTED
MIC Tap MIC to activate your microphone and start the visualizer. The red dot in the header pulses while active. Tap again to stop.
FULL Tap FULL to hide the header and controls. Tap anywhere on the visualizer to briefly show them again.
SET Opens the full settings panel where all parameters can be adjusted.
SPOTIFY Connect your Spotify account to show the current track, artist, and album art. See the Spotify Setup section below.
TOUCH GESTURES
Drag directly on the visualizer to adjust parameters in real time — a HUD briefly shows the current value. Gestures are ignored when the settings panel is open.
☝ ↕
GAIN
1 finger — drag up / down anywhere on screen
☝ ↔
MIN / MAX FREQ
1 finger — drag left / right. Left half of screen = Min Freq; right half = Max Freq
✌ ↕
NOISE FLOOR
2 fingers — slide both fingers up / down together
✌ ↔
BAR COUNT
2 fingers — pinch / spread horizontally (wider = more bars)
✌ ⇕
Y-SCALE
2 fingers — pinch / spread vertically (taller spread = higher scale, taller peaks)
VISUALIZATION
MODE Blocks Mirror — symmetric bars from centre. Blocks — bars rising from the bottom. Radial — circular sunburst pattern. Single H — one row of tall bricks across the screen. Single V — one column of bricks down the screen.
COLOR THEME Fire / Ice / Plasma / Acid / Mono — gradient colour schemes. Spectrum — rainbow colours per height. Multi — each bar gets its own hue. Album Art — palette extracted automatically from the Spotify track art.
PEAK HOLD Holds a tick mark at the highest recent value for each bar, making peaks easier to read at a glance.
BG BLUR When Spotify is connected, blurs the album art behind the visualizer as an ambient background wash.
NIGHT MODE Smooth flowing animation with no bright highlight caps on the bars — easier on the eyes in a dark room.
AUDIO
GAIN Amplifies the raw microphone signal (0.5× – 10×). Raise it if the bars look flat; lower it if they constantly max out. Also adjustable via the slider in the bottom bar or by dragging up/down on screen.
Y-SCALE Applies a curve to bar heights — higher values make quiet bars short and loud bars very tall, increasing visual contrast between soft and loud moments.
NOISE FLOOR Cuts constant background hum (0–100%). Raise it until bars go silent when music stops. Use Auto Calibrate to set this automatically.
SMOOTHING Blends frames together over time. Higher = slow, flowing movement. Lower = snappy, immediate response. Tune to taste.
FREQUENCY RANGE
MIN FREQ The lowest frequency displayed (0–200 Hz). Raise to ~80 Hz to cut low-end rumble or kick-drum dominance.
MAX FREQ The highest frequency displayed (5–35 kHz). Lower to 10–12 kHz to remove high-frequency hiss from the display.
FREQ SCALE Log and SQRT give more visual space to bass frequencies — most natural for music. Linear spreads all frequencies equally, emphasising highs.
BARS Number of frequency bands shown (8–128). More bars = finer frequency detail. Fewer bars = bolder, chunkier blocks. Adjust by pinching horizontally on screen.
AUTO CALIBRATE
Found in Settings — automatically sets Gain, Noise Floor and Y-Scale for your environment. Mic must be on first. The whole process takes about 12 seconds.
PHASE 1 — NOISE Stay silent for 5 seconds. Vusic measures the ambient hum of your room and sets the Noise Floor accordingly.
PHASE 2 — GAIN Play music at your normal listening volume for 5 seconds. Vusic sets Gain and Y-Scale so the bars fill the display well. Tap Apply when done.
FULLSCREEN OVERLAY
TRACK SIZE Controls how large the Spotify track info appears in fullscreen mode: Small, Med, or Large. You can also cycle through sizes by tapping the album art thumbnail in fullscreen.
POSITION Sets which corner (or centre) the track name and album art appear in during fullscreen.
SPOTIFY SETUP
Connecting Spotify shows the current track, artist, and album art in real time. When the Album Art colour theme is selected, Vusic automatically changes the palette to match the cover art.
1Go to developer.spotify.com and sign in with your Spotify account.
2Click Create app — give it any name and description.
3Under Redirect URIs add exactly:
https://ankharrufa.github.io/Vusic/
4Save the app, then copy the Client ID shown on the app dashboard.
5In Vusic, tap SET → scroll to the Spotify section → paste the Client ID into the field.
6Tap the green SPOTIFY button in the header. You will be redirected to Spotify to authorise, then returned to Vusic automatically.
Control Vusic from your phone using Firebase as a free relay — works over the internet, no shared WiFi needed. Others sharing the app are unaffected; only your devices connect to your database.
1Go to firebase.google.com and sign in with your Google account.
2Click Add project. Give it any name (e.g. "vusic"). You can skip Google Analytics.
3In the left menu go to Build → Realtime Database. Click Create Database → pick any location → choose Start in test mode → click Enable.
4Click the ⚙ gear icon (top-left) → Project settings. Scroll to Your apps → click the </> Web icon → enter any nickname → click Register app.
5Copy the firebaseConfig object shown on screen — it looks like { apiKey: "...", databaseURL: "...", ... }
6In Vusic tap SET → Remote Control → paste the config → tap SAVE. Status should turn green.
7Tap SHOW QR CODE FOR REMOTE. Scan it with your phone — the remote opens automatically pre-configured.
